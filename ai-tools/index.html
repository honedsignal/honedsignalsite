What it does: Verified on docs.rs (the rigspark-cli documentation, read November 11, 2026): every model gets a yes / slow / no verdict for your RAM and GPU with the reason, plus its download size and memory need before you download; you can paste any Hugging Face repository and it sizes each version and picks the largest that fits. What it costs: Verified on docs.rs and its Product Hunt listing: free — "RigSpark Community is free, MIT-licensed and built in the open" — with no account, no cloud and nothing metered. The real costs are the hardware you already own and the model downloads themselves.
