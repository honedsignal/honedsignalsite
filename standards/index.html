The standard

How every report is built

  • Source everything.

    Claims start from primary sources — court filings, official data, company documents, and the maker’s own site for prices. A source is provenance, not proof: that someone said a thing is not the same as the thing being true.

  • Verify claims.

    Findings are checked against the underlying source before they are published — including findings supplied by other AI systems, which are audited against the record, never adopted on trust.

  • Examine the evidence.

    Reports grade what they find: what is documented, what is alleged, what the data shows, and what is still unknown. The grades appear in the piece, not just in our notes.

  • Separate facts from opinions.

    Interpretation is labeled as interpretation. Verdicts — in reports and in the AI Tool Briefs — show their reasoning and the condition that would change them.

  • Disclose uncertainty.

    What a report could not confirm is printed in the report. AI involvement is disclosed, not hidden: bylines credit Muse, and artwork provenance is itemized where artwork appears, as on the Rio page.

  • Correct mistakes.

    When something published here is wrong, we fix it in place and record the change in the corrections log below — dated, with what changed and why. Substantive corrections are never made silently.

Method

SIFT, and a second reader

Honed Reports are built on the SIFT method — Stop, Investigate the source, Find better coverage, Trace claims to their original context — created by Mike Caulfield for exactly the kind of claim-checking these reports do.

Before a report is published, its draft is proofread by an independent AI reader (Claude), and the finished page is proofed again after it is built. Every item raised is audited against the source before any change is made; the editor rules on judgment calls. The tools follow the same discipline in their own way: every calculator prints its assumptions and carries a not-advice disclaimer, and the Site Scan publishes its own score on this site daily.

Independence

Who pays for Honed Signal

Honed Signal has no sponsors and runs no advertising. Nobody pays for coverage, placement, or a verdict: no one pays for a calculator’s results, and the AI Tool Briefs grade tools Honed Signal does not sell.

The site is funded by Freddie himself and by sales of Honed Signal’s own products — the printable packs, scan plans, and books sold through Etsy and Amazon. Links to Etsy and Amazon on this site go to Honed Signal's own products, and checkout happens there, never on this site. If an affiliate link is ever used on a page, it will be disclosed on that page.

Found something wrong?

Report an error

Email contact@honedsignal.com with the page and the claim you’re questioning. Every report is checked against its source. If we’re wrong, the page is fixed and the correction is logged below; if we’re not, we’ll show you the source we relied on.
