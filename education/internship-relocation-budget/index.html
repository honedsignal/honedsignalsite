The budget

Weekly spending

Budgeted food + other costs, per week:

—

Earnings = hourly pay × hours/week × weeks, plus any bonus or stipend. Costs = deposit + rent × months + travel + (food + other) × months. Surplus = earnings + current savings − costs. Weekly spending = budgeted food + other costs converted to a weekly figure; the cushion is what's left per week beyond the budget. Gross pay — taxes will take a bite.

Assumptions: Simplified budget: gross pay, no taxes withheld, no loan interest. Verify the offer letter — some 'stipends' replace hourly pay.
