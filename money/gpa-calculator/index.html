Explain these results

Your semester GPA is a weighted average. A course with more credit hours has more influence than a course with fewer hours.

Semester GPA = total quality points ÷ total semester credits

If you add your current GPA and completed credits, the calculator combines your earlier quality points with this semester’s quality points.

A+ / A = 4.0A− = 3.7B+ = 3.3B = 3.0 B− = 2.7C+ = 2.3C = 2.0C− = 1.7 D+ = 1.3D = 1.0D− = 0.7F = 0.0
