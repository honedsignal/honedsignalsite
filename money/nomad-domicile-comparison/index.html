Establishing domicile: the checklist

  • Get a driver’s license in the new state — usually requires an in-person visit.
  • Register and retitle your vehicle(s) there; fees and inspections vary by state.
  • Register to vote at your new domicile address.
  • Set up a mail-forwarding service (typically $100–$250/yr) and use that address everywhere.
  • Update insurance, banking, and medical records to the new address.
  • Keep proof of intent: nights spent in-state, club memberships, and where you file taxes.
  • State quirks: South Dakota has historically required just one overnight stay to claim residency; Texas and Florida require in-person visits for license and registration.

Not tax or legal advice

These are rough 2026 estimates for comparison only — registration fees, insurance bands, and mail-service prices change and vary by county, vehicle, and driving record. Domicile is a legal and tax concept: choosing the wrong state or doing it halfway can create real tax and insurance problems. Verify everything with a tax professional before you switch. Planning note: Texas, South Dakota, and Florida all have no state income tax on wages — that is why nomads favor them. The differences show up in vehicle registration, insurance costs, and health-plan networks. Your driving record, exact vehicle, and county can move these numbers more than the state choice itself.
