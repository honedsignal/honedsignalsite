Fuel is only part of owning a car. This calculator counts just the fuel (or electricity) for your commute — it leaves out depreciation, insurance, maintenance and repairs, tolls, parking, and financing (see “What is excluded” below). Your true cost per mile of driving is higher.

Small per-mile amounts compound fast over a commute: a few cents per mile, times hundreds of miles a week, becomes thousands of dollars a year. That’s why even a 10% cut in driving — one carpool day, one work-from-home day — shows up as real money.

Method

  • Annual miles = round-trip miles × days per week × weeks per year.
  • Gas: gallons used = annual miles ÷ MPG; annual fuel cost = gallons × price per gallon.
  • Electric: kWh used = annual miles ÷ miles-per-kWh; annual cost = kWh × price per kWh.
  • Cost per mile = annual fuel cost ÷ annual miles.
  • Monthly cost = annual fuel cost ÷ 12.
  • Drive-10%-less savings = annual fuel cost × 0.10.

Money values are rounded to two decimals; miles to whole numbers.

What is excluded

This calculator covers fuel or electricity only. It does not include depreciation, insurance, maintenance and repairs, tolls, parking, or financing costs.
