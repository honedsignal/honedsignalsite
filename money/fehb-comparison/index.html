What the numbers say

Planning note: FEHB premiums are shared — the federal government pays roughly 70% of the total premium, so the premium you enter is only your employee share. This calculator models premiums plus your out-of-pocket cost (deductible, then coinsurance, capped at the out-of-pocket max); it does not model copays, dental/vision (FEDVIP), prescriptions tiers, or network differences. Compare plans each Open Season at FEHB.gov.
