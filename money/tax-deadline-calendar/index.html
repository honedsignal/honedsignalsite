Why some 2028 dates move

IRS deadlines roll forward when the usual date lands on a weekend or legal holiday. The January payment and April filing dates each cross more than one non-business day.

Before you rely on a date

This calendar is built for the common case. A few circumstances can change your federal deadline.

Disaster relief

The IRS may postpone deadlines for people and businesses in federally declared disaster areas.

Living or serving abroad

Taxpayers outside the United States and qualifying military personnel can have different filing rules.

Fiscal-year filers

These dates assume a January–December tax year. A different tax year produces different due dates.

How these dates were checked

The 2026 schedule uses the IRS’s published 2026 guidance. The 2027 planning dates apply the IRS’s standing due-date rules to the verified 2027–2028 weekend and federal-holiday calendar. Recheck for later IRS relief or calendar updates before filing.
