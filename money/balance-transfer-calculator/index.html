Explain these results

Assumptions: This is a simple same-balance comparison. It estimates interest on the full starting balance for the introductory period, with no payments, new purchases, compounding, or rate changes. Your real cost will usually be lower if you pay the balance down. It does not include the regular APR after the offer ends.

For informational and educational purposes only. This calculator provides an estimate, not financial advice or a credit offer. Confirm the fee, eligible transfer amount, deadline, introductory term, and regular APR with the card issuer.
