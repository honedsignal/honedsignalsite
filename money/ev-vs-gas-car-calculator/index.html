How fuel costs are calculated

Annual mileage is divided by each vehicle’s efficiency, then multiplied by the relevant energy price.

miles ÷ efficiency × energy price

What this comparison leaves out

Insurance, maintenance, taxes, financing, charging equipment, incentives, resale value, and changing energy prices are not included. Treat this as a fuel-cost comparison—not a complete ownership-cost estimate.
