    Planning note: This is a simplified estimate of the 2026–27 Student Aid Index formula: parent income minus allowances (federal tax, payroll tax, a flat state-tax allowance, income protection, and a work allowance), assessed on a progressive 22%–47% scale; parent countable assets at 12%; student income minus a $11,770 protection allowance at 50%; student assets at 20%. The SAI cannot go below −$1,500. Retirement accounts and home equity are excluded. The real FAFSA at StudentAid.gov is the authority — filing it is free, and many schools and states require it for their own aid.
