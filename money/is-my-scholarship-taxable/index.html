Tax note: Scholarship taxability follows IRS Publication 970. Amounts used for tuition, required fees, and required books, supplies, and equipment are generally tax-free for degree-seeking students; room, board, travel, and personal expenses are generally taxable. This calculator estimates taxable scholarship income, not your full tax bill — it does not apply tax rates, credits, deductions, or state rules. Rules shown are as of 2026; tax law changes, so verify with IRS.gov or a tax professional.
