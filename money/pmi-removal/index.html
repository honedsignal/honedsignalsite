Your next move

Planning note: PMI rules come from the federal Homeowners Protection Act: you can request cancellation at 80% loan-to-value based on the original home value, and it ends automatically at 78% if you are current on payments. Your servicer may require a good payment history and proof the home has not lost value. These rules cover conventional loans — FHA mortgage insurance (MIP) works differently.
