    Explain these results

    This total is a planning estimate, not a provider quote. It simply adds the amounts you entered. A $0 line means no amount was entered for that item.

    Ask providers for an itemized price list so you can compare the same services. Some charges may come from separate businesses, such as the cemetery, crematory, florist, newspaper, clergy, or reception venue.

    Before agreeing to anything, check whether a prepaid plan, insurance policy, veterans benefit, membership benefit, or family burial plot may cover part of the cost.
