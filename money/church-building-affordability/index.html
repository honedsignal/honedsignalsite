The 30% rule of thumb

Lenders and church consultants usually want total facility costs near 30–35% of annual giving. Above 40%, one bad giving year becomes a crisis.

Note: This calculator compares facility costs against giving revenue; it does not account for loan fees, renovation overruns, or giving volatility.
