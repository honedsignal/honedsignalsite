Can I afford this car?

A car drains money four ways: the payment, insurance, fuel, and repairs. Most buyers only look at the first one. These calculators show you all four before you sign.

2

Totals insurance, fuel, maintenance, and depreciation — the real cost of owning.

Car Ownership Cost Calculator →

What to do with it: This number is often double the payment alone. Let it sink in.

3

Check it against your actual budget

Shows whether the total car cost fits your real monthly spending.

Monthly Budget Planner →

What to do with it: If the car breaks the budget, the answer is a cheaper car — not a longer loan.

Putting it together

You can afford the car if the full ownership cost fits your budget with room to spare, the loan term is reasonable, and you're not raiding your emergency fund for the down payment.
