Should I pay off debt or invest?

Every dollar has two possible jobs: killing debt or growing investments. The right choice depends on what your debt costs versus what investing might earn. These calculators make the comparison concrete.

Putting it together

Simple rule that holds up: if your debt charges more than about 7–8% interest, kill the debt first — that's a guaranteed return no investment can promise. Below that, splitting between debt and investing is reasonable.
