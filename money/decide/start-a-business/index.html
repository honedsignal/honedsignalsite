Should I start a business?

Most businesses fail on math, not effort. Before you leap, know exactly how much you must sell, how long your money lasts, and what to charge. These three calculators give you that.

1

Learn how much you must sell to survive

Calculates the sales volume where the business stops losing money.

Break-Even Calculator →

What to do with it: If you can't see a realistic path to this number, the idea needs work.

2

Know how long your money lasts

Shows how many months your savings cover the business before it must pay for itself.

Cash Runway Calculator →

What to do with it: Your runway must be longer than your path to break-even. Otherwise you're planning to crash.

3

Figure out what to charge

Works backward from the income you need to the rate you must charge.

Freelance Rate Calculator →

What to do with it: Most new businesses undercharge. This keeps you honest.

Putting it together

Start when you can see a credible path to break-even inside your cash runway, at prices customers will actually pay. If the math doesn't work on paper, it won't work in the real world either.
