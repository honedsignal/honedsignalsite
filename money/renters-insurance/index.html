What’s covered, and what isn’t

    Planning note: Renters insurance typically costs $15–$20 per month nationally and covers theft, fire, vandalism, and certain water damage (like burst pipes), plus liability for injuries to others and additional living expenses if your place becomes unlivable. It does not cover floods or earthquakes, which need separate policies. Your landlord’s insurance covers the building, not your belongings. Coverage amounts and exclusions vary by insurer — read the declarations page before you buy.
