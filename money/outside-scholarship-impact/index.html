Before you celebrate

Policies differ by school, and they are not always written down.

Ask the financial aid office which type of aid your award would replace.

Planning note: Schools generally cannot let total aid exceed the cost of attendance, so a new outside scholarship can cause "displacement" — the school reduces something else. Replacing a loan with a scholarship saves you interest; replacing a grant just moves money around. Rules vary by school and change over time.
