Planning note: Financing a medical bill with an interest-bearing plan means every payment splits between principal and interest, so the total you pay exceeds the bill itself. Many hospitals and providers offer 0% interest payment plans if you ask before agreeing to financed terms. Interest calculations here use standard monthly amortization on the bill amount you enter.
