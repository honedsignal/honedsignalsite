2026 state-by-state reference

Does your state tax personal income?

A plain-English directory of all 50 states. The green entries do not have a broad-based individual income tax on wages and salaries.

What “broad-based” means

It means the state generally taxes personal income such as wages and salaries. “No” does not mean tax-free: a state may still collect sales, property, business, local, or special taxes.

* Washington

Washington does not tax wages or salaries through a broad-based personal income tax. It does impose an excise tax on certain long-term capital gains.

New Hampshire changed in 2025

New Hampshire’s tax on interest and dividends was repealed for tax periods beginning on or after January 1, 2025.

Sources and scope

This directory classifies whether each state has a broad-based individual income tax on wages and salaries. It does not compare tax rates, local taxes, retirement-income rules, deductions, credits, residency, or filing requirements.
