The giver's side

You owe nothing on a real gift — but the giver may need to file Form 709 if they gave you more than $19,000 this year.

$19,000 per person

The 2026 annual exclusion. Above it: paperwork, not necessarily tax.

Tax note: Genuine gifts are not income to the recipient under IRC §102. The giver — not you — handles gift tax: up to $19,000 per recipient in 2026 needs no reporting at all, and tax is only owed after the lifetime exemption is exhausted. Payments for work, tips, and prizes are not gifts. Rules shown are as of 2026; verify with IRS.gov or a tax professional.
