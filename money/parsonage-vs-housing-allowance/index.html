The pastor's view

Pastors often prefer the allowance: it builds equity in their own home and moves with them. A parsonage is tax-efficient but ties housing to the job.

Tax note: Both a church-owned parsonage and a cash housing allowance are excludable from income tax under IRC §107 — but the allowance is capped at the lowest of designated amount, actual expenses, and fair rental value, while a parsonage's full rental value is excluded. Neither escapes self-employment tax unless Form 4361 was filed. Rules shown are as of 2026; verify with IRS.gov or a tax professional.
