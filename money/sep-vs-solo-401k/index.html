The verdict

Planning note: SEP-IRA contributions are employer-only (that's you, as the business owner), with no catch-up contributions at 50+. A Solo 401(k) lets you contribute as both employee and employer, offers a Roth option and catch-up contributions, but requires no employees besides a spouse — and generally needs a Form 5500-EZ once assets top $250,000. Figures use 2026 IRS limits; verify your plan documents before contributing.
