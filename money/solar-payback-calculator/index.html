Explain these results

  • We subtract your expected tax credit from the system cost.
  • We treat your current monthly electric bill as the amount solar replaces each month.
  • Break-even is the net cost divided by one year of estimated bill savings.
  • The 25-year figure is 25 years of estimated bill savings minus the net system cost.

This simple estimate does not include financing costs, maintenance, panel output changes, utility rate changes, fixed utility charges, or the time value of money.
