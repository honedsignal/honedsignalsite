The verdict

Planning note: The "4% rule" comes from the 1998 Trinity study: withdraw 4% in year one, then adjust that amount for inflation each year, and a 50/50 stock-and-bond portfolio historically survived 30 years. It is a rule of thumb, not a guarantee — sequence of returns, taxes, and fees all change the outcome, and longer retirements may need a lower rate.
