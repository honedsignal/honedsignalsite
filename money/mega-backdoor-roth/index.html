Your action checklist

The mega backdoor only works if your plan allows after-tax contributions. Confirm each step with your plan administrator.

  1. 1

    Check: does your plan allow after-tax contributions?

    Not all 401(k) plans do. Ask HR or your plan administrator — if the answer is no, the mega backdoor is off the table.

  2. 2

    Contribute after-tax up to your room

    Put in up to your after-tax room as non-Roth after-tax contributions. This is separate from your $24,500 elective limit.

  3. 3

    Convert to Roth inside the plan, fast

    Ask for an in-plan Roth conversion of the after-tax money. Many plans offer automatic daily conversions — use it. Any earnings before the conversion are taxable, so speed matters.

  4. 4

    Watch for plan-specific caps

    Highly compensated employees can face nondiscrimination (ACP) testing limits. And some plans cap after-tax contributions below the IRS ceiling.
