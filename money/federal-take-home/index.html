The 5% rule

Planning note: FERS rates are 0.8% (hired before 2013), 3.1% (hired in 2013), or 4.4% (hired 2014 or later) of basic pay. TSP and FEHB premiums are pre-tax and lower your federal taxable income; FERS deductions are after-tax. State tax is a flat-rate estimate — real state taxes vary widely. The 2026 TSP elective limit is $24,500 ($32,500 at 50+, $35,750 at 60–63).
