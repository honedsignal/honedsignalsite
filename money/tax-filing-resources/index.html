Which route fits?

Start with the shape of your return—not a software brand. Then confirm that the option supports every form you need before entering your tax information.

1

Simple W-2 return

Start with IRS Free File if your 2025 AGI is $89,000 or less. Above that, Fillable Forms handles federal filing at any income, but it is more hands-on and does not include a state return.

Eight private-sector partners participate for the 2026 filing season. Each offer has its own eligibility rules; many include a free state return, but not all.

More manual

Free File Fillable Forms

Electronic versions of federal tax forms for people comfortable preparing their own return. There is no income limit.

$0federal filing

Federal only. No guided interview and no state return.

Direct File is not an option this season. The federal IRS Direct File program ended before the 2026 filing season. Do not confuse it with state programs such as Direct File Oregon or Wisconsin’s WisTax.

Free help from people

Tax-preparation sites are seasonal. Call or check the locator before traveling, especially outside February through April.

VITA / TCE

IRS-certified volunteer help. VITA generally focuses on people around $70,244 or less, people with disabilities, and limited-English speakers. TCE focuses on age 60+ and retirement questions.

Locator: Find a site ↗
Phone: 800-906-9887

AARP Foundation Tax-Aide

Free help with an emphasis on taxpayers over 50 with low-to-moderate income. No AARP membership is required. Complex business, rental, and AMT returns may be out of scope.

Closed for the 2026 season; typically Feb. 1–Apr. 15.
Program details ↗

Taxpayer Advocate Service

Free, independent help within the IRS when a tax problem is causing financial difficulty or normal IRS channels have not resolved it.

Visit TAS ↗
Phone: 1-877-777-4778

Bring the basics. Photo ID; Social Security or ITIN cards for everyone on the return; birth dates; W-2s, 1099s and other income statements; last year’s federal and state returns if available; and bank routing/account numbers for direct deposit.
