Explain these results

What the two systems change

High-3 uses 2.5% for each year of creditable service. BRS uses 2.0%, so its pension portion is smaller for the same service and pay. BRS also includes government TSP matching and other benefits that are not included in this estimate.

What this estimate leaves out

This is a simple active-duty pension estimate. It does not model Reserve or Guard point calculations, disability retirement, cost-of-living adjustments, taxes, Survivor Benefit Plan premiums, VA offsets, or concurrent receipt rules.
