What this reserve is meant to cover

Roof and exteriorRoofing, siding, paint, gutters, windows, and weather-related wear.
Heating and coolingService, repairs, and eventual replacement of major HVAC equipment.
Plumbing and electricalLeaks, fixtures, outlets, panels, wiring, and other working systems.
Routine upkeepSmall repairs, preventive service, and replacement of worn home components.

Method: The calculator starts with 1% of current home value per year, then uses 1.00% for homes 0–5 years old, 1.10% for 6–15 years, 1.25% for 16–30 years, 1.50% for 31–50 years, and 1.75% for homes over 50 years. These are planning assumptions, not inspection findings.

Keep separate: This estimate does not include mortgage payments, property tax, homeowners insurance, utilities, renovations, or damage covered by insurance. Informational purposes only.
