Explain these results

What the total means

The middle estimate combines a rough attorney cost, executor compensation, and court or administrative costs. It is a planning figure—not a quote. In some states, attorney or executor compensation follows a fee schedule; in others, reasonable hourly fees are more common.

Why there is a range

Even an ordinary probate can cost more or less depending on local filing fees, the number and type of assets, required notices, appraisals, creditor claims, and whether anyone contests the will.

What is not included

This estimate does not include debts owed by the estate, taxes, real estate commissions, repairs, property carrying costs, or losses caused by a delayed sale. Those items reduce what beneficiaries may receive but are not all probate fees.

What may avoid probate

Assets with a valid beneficiary designation, many jointly owned assets, and assets already held in a properly funded trust may pass outside probate. State law controls, so review the estate with a licensed attorney in the state where the person lived.
