How it works

Risk budget = account size × risk percentage. Risk per share = the distance between the entry and stop prices. The calculator divides the budget by that distance, rounds down to a whole share, and caps the result at what the account can buy without borrowing.

Know the limits

  • Real losses can be larger if price gaps past your stop.
  • Fees, taxes, slippage, and existing positions are not included.
