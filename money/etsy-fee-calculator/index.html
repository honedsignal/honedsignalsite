Digital math is different math. No shipping means no fees on postage — but the flat $0.25 processing fee and the $0.20 listing charge land on every sale, so they bite hardest on cheap downloads. A $3 item and a $30 item pay the same $0.45 in flat charges.

Method: Percentage fees — transaction, payment processing, regulatory operating, and Offsite Ads — are charged on the price the buyer actually pays, after any sale discount. The flat $0.25 processing fee and the listing charge ($0.20 when the item sells, plus $0.20 for every 4 months the listing sits unsold) are counted per sale.
