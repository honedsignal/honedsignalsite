Planning note: Limits shown are 2026 IRS figures (Notice 2025-67). The 457(b) limit is separate from the 401(k)/403(b) limit, so one person can defer to both. Governmental 457(b) plans have no 10% early-withdrawal penalty after you separate from service — unlike 401(k)/403(b) plans. Non-governmental 457(b) plans follow different distribution rules; confirm your plan type with HR. A special pre-retirement catch-up can allow up to double the normal 457(b) limit in your last 3 working years. Employer match is modeled as dollar-for-dollar up to the entered percent of salary, simplified.
