Your monthly payment is the amount due each month. The amount financed is what remains after your down payment and trade-in. Total interest is the cost of borrowing that money, while total cost combines the vehicle price with all loan interest. A longer term may lower the monthly bill, but it usually increases the interest paid overall.

What this estimate includes

The calculator subtracts your down payment and trade-in from the vehicle price, then applies the APR across the loan term using a standard fixed-payment amortization formula. Taxes, registration, dealer fees, add-ons, rebates, and an existing trade-in loan balance are not included.
