Explain these results

Before you hand in a new W-4

This calculator is a simple one-job estimate. Verify the result with the IRS Tax Withholding Estimator—especially if you have multiple jobs, a working spouse, bonuses, itemized deductions, retirement income, tips, overtime, or investment income.

What’s included

2026 federal ordinary-income brackets, the 2026 standard deduction, pre-tax payroll deductions, and estimated child/other-dependent credits.

What’s excluded

Payroll taxes, state tax, EITC, AMT, itemized deductions, special OBBB deductions, spouse or second-job income, investment income, and tax already withheld earlier this year.
