What this comparison assumes

Monthly deposits are made at the end of each month and returns compound monthly. The lump-sum side assumes you already have the full planned contribution amount available on day one. Actual investment returns vary, and this estimate does not include taxes, fees, inflation, or changing contribution amounts.
