Why this amount? Umbrella insurance adds liability protection after the liability limits on an auto or homeowners policy are exhausted. Many insurance professionals use a range of 1 to 2 times net worth as a reference. It can also help address the risk that a court judgment reaches future wages after a serious lawsuit.

Your total exposed assets are the three amounts you entered added together. The recommendation rounds that total up to the next $1 million because umbrella policies are commonly sold in million-dollar increments, with a $1 million minimum in this estimate. The 2× figure is a higher reference point based only on your net worth. A licensed insurance professional can help account for your policy limits, household risks, state law, and which assets may already be protected.

Avoid double-counting

If your net-worth number already includes home equity, savings, or investments, enter those additional fields carefully. This calculator adds all three entries exactly as provided.

Coverage starts above your base limits

An umbrella policy generally sits on top of qualifying auto or homeowners liability coverage. Insurers may require minimum limits on those underlying policies.
