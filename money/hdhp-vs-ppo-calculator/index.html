How the estimate works

  1. You pay the entered medical costs up to the plan’s deductible.
  2. Above the deductible, you pay the entered coinsurance percentage until the out-of-pocket maximum is reached.
  3. Annual premiums are added to both plans. For the HDHP, estimated tax savings equal to 22% of the HSA contribution are subtracted.
