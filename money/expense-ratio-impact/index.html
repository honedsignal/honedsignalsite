The eye-opener

Fee note: An expense ratio is the yearly percentage a fund skims off your investment. A 1.00% fee on a 7% return doesn't just take 1% of your money — it takes roughly 1% off your growth every single year, and the lost compounding adds up. This calculator uses smooth average returns and doesn't model taxes, fees charged outside the expense ratio, or market ups and downs.
