A three-year view

Years until care — Care age minus current age
Future monthly care cost — Today’s cost grown at 3% per year
Estimated three-year total — Future monthly cost × 36 months
Monthly savings needed now — Straight-line estimate; ignores investment growth

Here’s the simple version: this starts with the monthly care price you entered, then raises it by 3% for every year until the age you selected. That gives the estimated monthly cost when care begins.

The three-year total assumes that same future monthly price continues for 36 months. The savings figure spreads that total evenly across every month between now and then. It does not include interest, investment gains, taxes, insurance benefits, or changes in the level of care—so use it as a planning starting point, not a prediction.

What the estimate assumes

Care costs rise 3% each year. Actual local costs can rise faster or slower, and your care needs may change over time.

The plan covers 36 months. You can use the result to compare with savings, long-term care insurance, family support, or other resources.

Savings grow in a straight line. The monthly amount does not assume any investment return, interest, fees, or taxes.
