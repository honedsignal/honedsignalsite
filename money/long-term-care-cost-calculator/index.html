What the benchmark includes

The built-in estimates use CareScout’s 2025 national median rates. Home caregiver assumes 40 hours a week; adult day health assumes 5 days a week. Residential settings use published monthly rates multiplied by 12.

What this estimate leaves out

  • Local price differences, taxes, and one-time fees
  • Policy benefit caps, waiting periods, exclusions, and premium costs
  • Medicare, Medicaid, family care, or other funding sources
