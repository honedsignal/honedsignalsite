Plain-English explanation

What lenders look for

DTI is one part of the decision. A lender may also weigh your credit history, the stability of your income, your down payment, cash reserves, and the rules for the loan you want. Different lenders and loan products set different limits, so this result estimates the math—not whether you will be approved.

Method: Front-end DTI = housing ÷ gross monthly income. Back-end DTI = housing plus other listed monthly debt ÷ gross monthly income. The 28/36 comparison is a traditional rule of thumb; it is not a legal limit.
