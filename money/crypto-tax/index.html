The holding-period edge

Planning note: Crypto held more than one year gets the 0%/15%/20% long-term capital gains rates; held one year or less, gains are taxed as ordinary income. Uses 2026 federal brackets and standard deductions. Wash-sale rules do not currently apply to crypto (as of 2026), though Congress has discussed changing this — selling at a loss and rebuying is not disallowed the way it is for stocks. State taxes and the 3.8% Net Investment Income Tax are not included.
