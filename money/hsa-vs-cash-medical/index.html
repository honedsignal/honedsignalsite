The pro move

Planning note: The HSA's triple tax advantage — tax-deductible contributions, tax-free growth, tax-free withdrawals for qualified medical expenses — makes it the only triple-tax-advantaged account in the tax code. Growth figures assume a steady average return; real markets vary. After age 65, HSA money can be withdrawn for any reason and taxed like a Traditional IRA, with no penalty.
