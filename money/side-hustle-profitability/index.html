The verdict

Planning note: Taxes apply to profit (income minus expenses), not to gross revenue. Self-employment income is generally subject to both income tax and self-employment tax, so a combined rate of 25–30% is a common estimate — your actual rate depends on your total income and filing situation. This tool does not subtract unpaid time such as marketing, admin, or commuting between gigs; the real hourly rate is often lower than shown.
