The five score factors

The percentages below are a common FICO framework. Your exact score can differ because lenders use different models and credit bureaus may hold different information.

35%

Payment history

Whether accounts are paid on time. Missed payments can matter for years.

30%

Amounts owed

How much available revolving credit you use. Lower is generally better.

15%

Credit age

How long your accounts have been open, including the average account age.

10%

New credit

Recent applications and newly opened accounts can temporarily weigh on a score.

10%

Credit mix

Experience handling different account types can help, but never borrow just for variety.
