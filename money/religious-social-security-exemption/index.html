Beyond Social Security

The 4029 exemption is the only true opt-out. But religion touches taxes in a few other places:

Worth knowing

Clergy housing allowance (§107) can be excluded from income tax · Ministers file Form 4361, not 4029 · Churches and ministries are tax-exempt as organizations under 501(c)(3) · Tithes and donations are deductible for the giver · Houses of worship may be exempt from property tax — rules vary by state.

Tax note: The religious exemption from Social Security and Medicare taxes comes from IRC §1402(g) and is claimed on IRS Form 4029. It applies only to Social Security and Medicare taxes — federal income tax is still owed in full. The exemption takes effect only after the IRS approves the application, and approval permanently waives the applicant's future Social Security and Medicare benefits. Ministers use Form 4361 instead, under different rules. This checker screens the published criteria; it is not a determination of eligibility.
