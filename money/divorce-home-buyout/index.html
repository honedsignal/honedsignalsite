Can you carry it?

Planning note: Most lenders like to see total debt-to-income at or below 43%, with housing costs at or below about 28–36% of income. This assumes you refinance the full mortgage balance plus the buyout into one new loan on your own. It does not include property tax or homeowners insurance, which make the real monthly cost higher. Talk with your attorney before agreeing to any split.
