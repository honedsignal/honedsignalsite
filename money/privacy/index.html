Privacy Policy

Honed Money is built to help you run the numbers without handing over your information.

The short version

Your calculator inputs stay on your device. There are no accounts, no signup, and no advertising trackers.

No account required

You can use Honed Money without creating an account or signing up. We do not ask for your name, email address, or financial account details to use the calculators.

Your calculations stay with you

Everything you type into a calculator stays in your browser. The math runs locally in JavaScript on your own device. Calculator inputs and results are never sent to or stored by Honed Money.

How “Email my results” works

The “Email my results” button opens your own email app using a mailto: link. Your email app prepares the message. Honed Money does not send, receive, or store the email or its contents.

No advertising trackers

Honed Money does not use tracking cookies for advertising. We do not build a profile of what you do across other websites.

Hosting and simple analytics

Cloudflare hosts the site and provides privacy-friendly analytics. These analytics help us understand basic site use without tracking people across websites.

We do not sell or share your data

Honed Money does not sell, rent, or share personal data with anyone. We do not send calculator inputs or results to advertisers, data brokers, or other businesses.

Privacy questions

If you have a question about this policy, please use the Honed Money contact page.
