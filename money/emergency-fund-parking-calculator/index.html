BEST OPTION AFTER TAXES

—

HIGH-YIELD SAVINGS——Instant access, no penalty. Fully liquid.
CERTIFICATE OF DEPOSIT——Locked for the CD term. Early withdrawal usually costs several months of interest.
TREASURY BILLS——Exempt from state income tax. Sellable on the secondary market, usually within a day or two.
MONEY MARKET——Check-writing and debit access at many banks. Fully liquid.
