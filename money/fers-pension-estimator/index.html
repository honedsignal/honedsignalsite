The 1.1% boost

Planning note: FERS annuities follow the OPM formula: 1.0% of your high-3 average salary for each year of creditable service, or 1.1% if you are age 62 or older with at least 20 years of service. Electing a survivor benefit reduces your annuity — 5% for a partial (25%) survivor annuity or 10% for a full (50%) one. Special groups (law enforcement, firefighters, air traffic controllers) use a different formula. Unused sick leave adds to creditable service for the computation. This is an estimate only; OPM is the final authority on your annuity.
