Tax-free dollars

Planning note: BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing) is not subject to federal income tax, so every dollar of it covers housing at full value — unlike taxable pay, where a chunk goes to taxes first. Rates are set by the DoD by pay grade, duty station, and dependency status, and change each January 1. This calculator covers rent and utilities only; it does not include renters or homeowners insurance, HOA dues, or maintenance.
