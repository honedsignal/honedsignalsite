The power of compounding

Planning note: This projection assumes a steady annual return and unchanged monthly savings, which real markets and real life will not deliver. It ignores inflation, taxes, and fees. Use a conservative return — 5% to 7% is a common planning range for a diversified portfolio, and 3% roughly approximates a savings account. The numbers show what compounding can do, not what it will do.
