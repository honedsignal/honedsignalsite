Assumptions behind these numbers
  • Interest is added monthly, before that month's payment, at balance × APR ÷ 12 — the same method the site's other debt tools use.
  • Estimated minimums are modeled at 2% of the current balance with a $25 floor, recalculated each month as the balance falls — the house model the Minimum Payment Trap uses, labeled wherever it applies. Minimums you type in are treated as fixed amounts.
  • The plan holds your monthly amount steady: every minimum gets paid first, then everything left goes to one target debt until it's gone — smallest balance first (Snowball) or highest APR first (Avalanche).
  • No new charges, no fees, APRs never change, and every payment is on time. Life rarely cooperates with all four; treat the dates as a map, not a promise.

For informational purposes only. Estimates assume rates and monthly payments stay the same, interest is added monthly, and no new charges or fees are added. Actual payoff timing may differ by lender.
