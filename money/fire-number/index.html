Planning note: The 4% rule comes from the 1998 Trinity study: withdraw 4% of your portfolio in year one, then adjust for inflation, and a stock-and-bond portfolio historically lasted 30 years. Retiring decades early is a longer horizon than the study tested — many early retirees plan around 3–3.5% instead. This calculator does not model taxes, fees, healthcare costs, or market crashes.
