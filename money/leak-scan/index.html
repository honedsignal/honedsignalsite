Assumptions behind these numbers
  • Every charge is annualized at its stated rate: weekly × 52, monthly × 12, quarterly × 4, every 6 months × 2, yearly × 1 — the same normalization the Subscription Cost Analyzer uses.
  • The zombie subtotal counts charges you marked "months ago" or "can't remember" for last use. It's a prompt to look, not a verdict — a charge can be worth keeping even if you haven't opened it lately.
  • The scan totals only what you entered. It cannot see charges you didn't list — annual renewals, app-store subscriptions, and converted free trials are the usual hiding places, and hunting those down is the paid plan's statement-audit checklist.
  • Prices are what you typed. Taxes, fees on fees, and mid-year price rises aren't modeled.
