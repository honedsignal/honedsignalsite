Explain these results

Estimated annual value adds base salary, expected bonus, the employer’s 401(k) match, and a dollar estimate for paid time off. It then subtracts your yearly health insurance premium.

The 401(k) estimate assumes you contribute enough to receive the full match. Paid time off is valued at salary ÷ 260 workdays × PTO days; it represents the value of paid time, not extra cash in your paycheck.

This is a first-pass comparison. Taxes, vesting schedules, deductibles, equity, commute costs, overtime, pension benefits, and quality of life can change the real winner.
