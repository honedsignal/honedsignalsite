Medicare doesn't cover care abroad. If you're 65+ and on Medicare, it covers you in the US — but not a hospital visit in another country. Add travel medical insurance for every international leg.

Network gap warning: many ACA plans (especially HMOs/EPOs) cover little outside your home state. If you roam the US, a PPO with a national network — or a plan paired with a multi-state network — matters more than the cheapest premium.
