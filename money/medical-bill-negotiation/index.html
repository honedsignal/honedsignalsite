Your action checklist

  • ✓Ask for an itemized bill. Review every line for duplicate charges and services you did not receive.
  • ✓Ask for a prompt-pay discount. Call the billing department and offer to pay the balance now for 20–40% off.
  • ✓Apply for financial aid. Non-profit hospitals must offer charity-care applications — ask for one before you pay.

Planning note: Hospitals negotiate bills every day — the first number on the statement is a starting point, not a final price. Actual discounts and aid vary by provider, and financial-aid eligibility depends on income and the hospital's policy. Estimates only, not financial or legal advice.
