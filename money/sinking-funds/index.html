The sinking fund idea

Planning note: A sinking fund is savings with a job: a known future expense gets its own bucket, and you feed it a little every month until the bill arrives. It is the opposite of putting a car repair or holiday trip on a credit card. Keep sinking funds in an accessible savings account, separate from your emergency fund if you can.
