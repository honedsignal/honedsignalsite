Withholding vs. reality

Planning note: Federal tax uses the 2026 IRS brackets and standard deduction, applied marginally to taxable income. Employers must withhold a flat 22% federal tax on supplemental wages like bonuses (up to $1M). Your actual federal tax is your marginal rate on the bonus — the difference settles on your tax return. FICA is 6.2% Social Security on wages up to $184,500 (2026 wage base) plus 1.45% Medicare on all wages, with an extra 0.9% Medicare tax above $200,000 ($250,000 married filing jointly). State tax uses the rate you enter, applied to the full bonus. Pre-tax deductions like 401(k) contributions are not included.
