Explain these results

  • COBRA usually keeps the same employer plan, provider network, and progress you already made toward its deductible.
  • Marketplace coverage is a new plan. Its provider network and drug list may differ, and its deductible usually starts over.
  • A deductible is not an automatic bill. It is generally what you pay for covered care before the plan begins sharing certain costs.
  • This comparison does not include copays, coinsurance, the COBRA plan’s deductible, or either plan’s out-of-pocket limit.
