How this estimate works

The starting dividend per share equals the entered share price multiplied by the starting yield. Once each year, the dividend per share changes by your growth rate. In the reinvest path, that year's dividend buys fractional shares at the entered share price; those new shares earn dividends in later years. In the cash path, the starting share count does not change and each year's dividend is added to cash. The share price itself does not grow in this estimate.
