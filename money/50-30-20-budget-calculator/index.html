Needs are bills you must pay. Wants are things you could cut back if needed. The final 20% goes to savings goals and debt payments above the minimum.

This rule is a guide, not a test. High housing, medical, caregiving, or transportation costs may push needs above 50%. Start with your real bills, then move the percentages in a direction that works for you.

How the math works: This calculator multiplies monthly after-tax income by 50%, 30%, and 20%. It does not account for taxes, payroll deductions, irregular income, or specific bills.
