The tradeoff

Planning note: Doctor loans (physician mortgages) are offered to MDs, DOs, dentists (DDS/DMD), veterinarians (DVM), and often residents and fellows — some lenders also include PAs and NPs. They typically allow 0–10% down with no PMI, but the rate often runs a bit higher than conventional and some are adjustable-rate or have loan caps. Doctor-loan lenders are also more flexible on student-loan debt in the DTI math (deferred loans may be excluded or counted at an income-driven payment). PMI on a conventional loan usually falls off at 20% equity; this comparison keeps it for the full term, so it slightly overstates the conventional cost. Shop both quotes with real lenders.
