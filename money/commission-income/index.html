The tax catch

Commission is usually withheld as supplemental wages — a flat federal rate that often differs from your paycheck withholding. Plan a tax buffer.

Tax note on irregular income: Commission payouts are typically withheld at the flat supplemental-wage rate (22% federal for most payouts, up to $1 million), which may be higher or lower than your normal paycheck withholding. Your actual tax is based on total annual income — a big commission year can push you into a higher bracket, so consider setting aside part of each check. Clawbacks, caps, and plan details vary by employer.
