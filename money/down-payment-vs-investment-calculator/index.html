Explain these results
How this estimate works: The down-payment path treats the extra cash as immediate home equity, compounds the avoided mortgage interest monthly, and adds the PMI entered for each month. The investment path compounds the same cash monthly at your expected return. It does not include taxes, fees, changing property values, investment volatility, mortgage payoff timing, or the possible investment of monthly savings.
This calculator is for informational and educational purposes only and does not constitute investment advice. Results are estimates, not guarantees. Consider liquidity, risk, taxes, and your full financial situation before deciding.
