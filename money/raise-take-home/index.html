Planning note: Federal tax uses the 2026 IRS brackets and standard deduction, applied marginally to taxable income. FICA is 6.2% Social Security on wages up to $184,500 (2026 wage base) plus 1.45% Medicare on all wages, with an extra 0.9% Medicare tax above $200,000 ($250,000 married filing jointly). State tax uses the rate you enter, applied to gross salary. Pre-tax deductions like 401(k) contributions, health premiums, and HSA contributions are not included.
