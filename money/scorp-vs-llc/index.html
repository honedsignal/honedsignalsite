Planning note: This is an estimate of payroll-tax differences only. Income tax is the same either way — both structures pass profits through to your personal return. Reasonable-salary rules are closely watched by the IRS; setting an artificially low salary to dodge payroll taxes can trigger audits and penalties. State fees and franchise taxes vary widely. Talk to a CPA before making the election.
