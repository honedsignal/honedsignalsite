    The takeaway

    Planning note: Budgeting to your worst month is the standard approach for gig, freelance, commission, and seasonal income. Treat one-time windfalls separately — spend the baseline, bank the surplus. Keep income estimates realistic; underestimating your low month is safer than overestimating it.
