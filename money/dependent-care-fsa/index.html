The play

Planning note: 2026 dependent care FSA limit is $7,500 per household ($3,750 if married filing separately), raised from $5,000 by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. Your employer's plan must be amended to allow the full amount — confirm with HR. FSA dollars are use-it-or-lose-it: spend them by year-end (or your plan's grace period) or they are forfeited. The Child and Dependent Care Credit caps eligible expenses at $3,000 (one dependent) or $6,000 (two or more) and can't double-dip FSA dollars. The credit is nonrefundable.
