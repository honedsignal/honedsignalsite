Important: This is an estimate only — not an eligibility determination. The Marketplace at healthcare.gov (or your state exchange) makes the official call using your Modified Adjusted Gross Income, which can differ from the income you enter here. Subsidy rules and percentages change every year; this page uses the 2026 schedule (IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-25). The temporary enhanced subsidies expired after 2025, so households above 400% of the poverty line are not eligible for a credit in 2026. If your income is below 138% of the poverty line you may qualify for Medicaid instead, depending on your state.
