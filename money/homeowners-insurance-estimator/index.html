Explain these results

Dwelling coverage

This is based on what rebuilding could cost, rounded up to the next $10,000. It is not based on your mortgage balance or sale price. Ask whether extended replacement coverage can add a cushion if labor and material costs jump.

Personal property coverage

This reflects the belongings value you entered, rounded up to the next $5,000. A room-by-room home inventory can reveal whether this amount is too low. Jewelry, art, and collectibles may need separate limits.

Personal liability coverage

This rounds your requested amount up to the next $100,000, with a $300,000 starting floor. Higher limits may fit households with more assets or added risks. An umbrella policy can provide protection above a home policy's limit.

What is not included

This tool does not estimate deductibles, temporary living expenses, detached structures, flood, earthquake, wind exclusions, or local code upgrades. Review those items separately before choosing a policy.
