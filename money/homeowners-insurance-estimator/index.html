Use the cost to rebuild the house—not its sale price or the value of the land.

What moves the estimate

  • 1
    The home itself

    A higher rebuild cost means the insurer could need to pay more after a covered total loss.

  • 2
    Local hazards

    More exposure to wildfire, hurricane, or hail generally pushes premiums higher.

  • 3
    Your deductible

    A higher deductible lowers this estimate because you agree to pay more before coverage begins.

Planning formula: 0.35% of rebuild cost, adjusted for risk and deductible.

Your estimate is shown as a range because insurance prices are not fixed from these three details alone. Think of the annual range as a useful amount to set aside in a yearly budget; the monthly range is that same cost divided across 12 months. It is not a quote.
