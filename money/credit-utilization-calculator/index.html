Timing matters: card issuers often report a statement balance to the credit bureaus. A payment may not appear in a credit report immediately, and credit scores also use factors beyond utilization.

How it works: utilization equals balance ÷ credit limit. Overall utilization uses all entered balances divided by all entered limits. Target payment figures show the amount needed to bring each card to 30% or 10% utilization.
