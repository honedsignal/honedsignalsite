Explain these results

One model, not your state’s rulebook

This tool uses a simplified income-shares approach. It estimates a combined child-rearing budget, assigns that budget by each parent’s share of income, then gives credit for each parent’s share of overnights. Actual guidelines vary by state and may add childcare, health insurance, medical costs, other children, existing support orders, taxes, low-income protections, and court-approved adjustments.

Method: Estimated child budget equals combined gross monthly income multiplied by 17% for one child, 25% for two, 29% for three, 31% for four, 34% for five, or 36% for six or more. The midpoint transfer compares each parent’s income share with parenting-time share; the displayed range is 15% below to 15% above that midpoint.
