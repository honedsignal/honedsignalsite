How the math works

Your match is your eligible contribution multiplied by the employer match rate. Eligible contributions stop at the plan’s “up to” percentage of salary. This calculator uses annual pay and does not model per-paycheck true-ups.

Check the plan details

Some plans use tiers, fixed-dollar amounts, waiting periods, vesting schedules, or paycheck-by-paycheck limits. If your formula has more than one tier, this simple calculator may not match your plan.
