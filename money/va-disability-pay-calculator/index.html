Explain these results

What’s included

Basic VA disability compensation for ratings from 10% to 100%, plus eligible spouse, parent, child, school-child, and spouse Aid and Attendance additions reflected in the 2026 table.

What’s not included

Special Monthly Compensation, unemployability, apportionment, offsets, or unique award details. Your VA decision letter controls your actual payment.

Rates are a fixed snapshot of the official U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs 2026 compensation tables, effective December 1, 2025 and checked October 7, 2026.
