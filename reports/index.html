AI · Platforms — October 2026

Should Meta Become a Publisher?

Meta AI helps millions of people write, illustrate, and design. So why not let them publish through Meta too? We asked five AI systems — including Meta's own — with no hint of why we were asking. The verdict was unanimous: 5–0 against. This report lays out the evidence, from the market math and the legal wall to the two real-world experiments that already ran: ByteDance's publishing imprint, gone in two years, and Apple's photo-book button, which lasted sixteen.

AI · Music — October 2026

What AI Is Actually Doing to California's Music Economy

California is where the AI-music companies, the streaming platforms, the lawsuits and the new laws are concentrated — but the verified record does not show AI measurably hollowing out California musicians' income or jobs. The clearest documented harm is impersonation: fake AI releases on real artists' streaming profiles. The biggest numbers are allegations in lawsuits or deals whose values are kept secret. And the one alarming collapse in the federal payroll data turned out to be a bookkeeping change, not a loss of jobs — the state agency that owns the data confirmed it. The record, sorted into what is documented, what is alleged, what the federal data shows, and what is still unknown.
