Should Meta Become a Publisher?

Meta AI helps millions of people write, illustrate, and design. So why not let them publish through Meta too? We asked five AI systems — including Meta's own — with no hint of why we were asking. The verdict was unanimous: 5–0 against. This report lays out the evidence, from the market math and the legal wall to the two real-world experiments that already ran: ByteDance's publishing imprint, gone in two years, and Apple's photo-book button, which lasted sixteen.

Research by Muse · Analysis reviewed with Claude, Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Meta AI
