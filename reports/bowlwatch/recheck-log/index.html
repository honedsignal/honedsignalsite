THE CLAIM BEING RE-CHECKED (from the BowlWatch session record):

Purina says it removed artificial colors, yet Purina label PDFs captured Nov 15, 2026 for Dog Chow, Alpo, Friskies dry and Cat Chow still listed Red 40. The record flagged this: "needs exact re-capture before publishing."

PART 1 — WHAT PURINA / NESTLÉ ACTUALLY CLAIMED

A. Purina, Fancy Feast page:

https://www.purina.com/fancy-feast/no-artificial-colors Exact wording: "many of our recipes are now crafted without artificial preservatives or colors" — scope: Fancy Feast only, and only the listed recipes. NOT a Purina-wide pet food claim.

B. Nestlé USA press releases:

https://www.nestleusa.com/media/pressreleases/eliminate-use-fdc-colors Pledge: timeline to "fully eliminate FD&C colors in its U.S. food and beverage portfolio by mid-2026." https://www.nestleusa.com/media/pressreleases/removal-of-fdc-colors-across-us-portfolio Completion: "fully eliminated Food, Drug & Cosmetic (FD&C) colors from its U.S. food and beverage portfolio." Scope: Nestlé USA's HUMAN food and beverage portfolio. Purina pet foods are Nestlé Purina PetCare Company products and are not covered by the wording of either release.

PART 2 — THE FOUR PRODUCTS (Purina label PDFs, opened Nov 15, 2026)

1. Dog Chow — Dog Chow Little Bites for Small Dogs (code M412023)

https://www.purina.com/sites/default/files/product-label-deck-file/2024-05/4120-m412023-dog-chow-little-bites-for-small-dogs-dry-dog-food-lb1-1.pdf Ingredient snippet: "...biotin (Vitamin B-7)], Yellow 6, Yellow 5, Red 40, Blue 2, garlic oil." Verdict: Red 40 PRESENT

2. Alpo — ALPO Come & Get It! Cookout Classics (code L502324)

https://www.purina.com/sites/default/files/product-label-deck-file/2025-03/5023_l502324_alpo_come_and_get_it_dry_dog_food_894.pdf Ingredient snippet: "...L-Tryptophan, Red 40, Yellow 5, calcium carbonate, Blue 2, Yellow 6, garlic oil." Verdict: Red 40 PRESENT

3. Friskies (dry) — Purina Friskies Ocean Favorites Dry Cat Food (code A631919)

https://www.purina.com/sites/default/files/products/files/A631919_Friskies_Ocean_Favorites_Dry_Cat_Food.pdf Ingredient snippet: "...DL-Methionine, L-Tryptophan, Red 40, Blue 2, Yellow 5." Verdict: Red 40 PRESENT Corroborating variant — Friskies Farm Favorites Dry Cat Food (code A629918): "...DL-Methionine, L-Tryptophan, Red 40, Yellow 6, Yellow 5, Blue 2." Red 40 PRESENT

4. Cat Chow — Purina Cat Chow Brand Cat Food – Complete (code N450118)

https://purina.com/sites/default/files/products/files/450118N_Purina_Cat_Chow_Brand_Cat_Food_Complete.pdf Ingredient snippet: "...DL-Methionine, Red 40, Yellow 5, Blue 2." Verdict: Red 40 PRESENT

Note: the current Purina US product pages for Dog Chow Complete Adult Chicken and Cat Chow Complete were also opened on Nov 15, 2026, but their ingredient blocks did not render in text extraction, so the verdicts rest on the Purina label PDFs above, not on those pages. No product was blocked or unreadable; there are no "could not verify" entries.

OVERALL VERDICT

Ingredient half: HOLDS, 4 of 4. Purina's own currently hosted label information still lists Red 40 for Dog Chow, Alpo, Friskies dry and Cat Chow.

Removal-claim half: HOLDS ONLY AS SCOPED. Purina's claim covers many Fancy Feast recipes; Nestlé USA's covers its human food and beverage portfolio. Neither, as worded, covers these four pet foods. The report prints the narrowed version and claims no broken promise.
