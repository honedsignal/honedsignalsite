Privacy Policy

No hype. No noise.

What this site collects (very little), what it never collects, and who to contact. Last updated November 8, 2026.

The short version

Privacy in one paragraph

Honed Signal has no accounts and no sign-up to read the site or use the tools. The calculators and scans do their math in your browser. We use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits in aggregate, and the only personal information we ever receive is what you choose to send: an email address when you ask for something by email, or a message when you write to us. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data, and we run no advertising trackers.

On your device

The tools keep your numbers with you

Every calculator across Honed Money, Honed Flint, and Honed Proof runs locally in your browser. Inputs and results are never sent to us or stored by us. The Debt Scan and Leak Scan are built the same way on purpose: your figures never leave your device, and if you choose to unlock a plan, only the email address you enter for delivery is transmitted — not your numbers.

The free Site Scan works differently in one respect: to scan a website, the address you enter is sent to our scanning service, which fetches that site’s public pages. Don’t enter anything you wouldn’t put in a browser’s address bar.

Analytics & hosting

Counting visits, not people

Hosting. Cloudflare hosts this site and, like any host, keeps standard delivery and security logs.

Analytics. We use Ahrefs Web Analytics for aggregate traffic statistics — which pages get visited, in roughly what numbers. It uses no advertising cookies and does not build profiles of you across other websites.

Third-party pieces. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google’s servers receive that request, as with any embedded file. Where a video is embedded, it is a YouTube player, and YouTube’s own rules apply once you press play.

Email

Email only when you ask for it

Scan deliveries. If you enter an email address on a scan page, it passes through our automation provider (Make) solely to deliver what you asked for and keep a record of the request.

Free downloads. The Texas Wildlife Activity Pack list runs on MailerLite with double opt-in: you confirm by email before anything is sent, every message carries an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing ends it. Asking for one specific delivery never adds you to a general mailing list.

Writing to us. If you email us, we receive your address and your message and use them to reply. Reports of errors go through the same address — see the Standards & Corrections page.

Buying

Checkout happens elsewhere

Honed Signal takes no payments on this site and stores no card details. Products are sold through Etsy and Amazon; payment and delivery run under their privacy policies, and those platforms share with us the order details needed to deliver and support your purchase.

Your choices

Questions, access, deletion

Questions about this policy, or about an email record we hold for you, go to contact@honedsignal.com. Ask and we will tell you what we hold — in practice, an email address on a delivery or list record — and delete it on request. List emails can also be removed at any time through their unsubscribe link.

If this policy changes in substance, the change is posted here with a new “last updated” date. Honed Money’s own Privacy Policy covers that section in more detail and agrees with this page.
