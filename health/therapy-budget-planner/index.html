Cost levers you control

  • Ask about sliding-scale rates — many therapists adjust fees by income.
  • Check out-of-network reimbursement — compare paths with the Therapy Cost Comparer.
  • Sessions per month is a cost dial — fewer sessions lowers the total. How often to go is a clinical question for your provider, not a cost recommendation.

Discretionary income is take-home pay minus fixed expenses. Verdicts use rough bands: up to 10% of discretionary is a comfortable fit, up to 25% is workable, above 25% is tight. Bands are a budgeting shorthand, not financial advice. HSA/FSA math assumes the balance is used for therapy until it runs out; it doesn't change the sticker price, just the source of payment.
