Before you sign

  • Ortho benefits are lifetime, not annual. A $1,500 ortho max is once per person, ever — check how much is left before you plan.
  • Ask about the in-office payment plan. Many orthodontists split the balance interest-free over treatment.
  • FSA/HSA dollars are still your money — they're just pre-tax, so the real cost is a bit less than it looks.
  • Retainers and follow-up visits may bill separately from the quoted total.

The ortho benefit is applied as a flat lifetime maximum against the quoted total (real insurers often pay it out in installments, but the total is the same).
