How we grade the evidence

Every review on this page gets one of four grades \u2014 for a specific use, not for a whole field. Safety is assessed separately from whether something works.

01

Well established

Strong, consistent evidence from high-quality studies. This is as close to settled as medicine gets.

02

Promising evidence

Real signals from decent studies, but not enough to be confident. Worth watching, not worth betting on.

03

Preliminary evidence

Early findings \u2014 small studies, animal or lab work, or mixed results. Interesting, not actionable.

04

Insufficient evidence

Too little reliable evidence to say either way. Absence of proof is not proof of absence \u2014 or of benefit.
