How to use this section

Medical news moves fast and headlines move faster. These pages give you the background knowledge to read health news like a skeptic: what the FDA process actually tests, how trials work, and a checklist for judging any "breakthrough" claim.

Nothing here is medical advice. These pages explain processes — how drugs get approved, how trials run, how evidence gets graded — not whether any specific treatment is right for you. For questions about your own care, talk to a qualified health professional. And when you're ready to judge whether something works, see how Honed Proof grades evidence.
