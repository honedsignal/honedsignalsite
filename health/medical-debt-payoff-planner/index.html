    This plan pays each debt’s monthly minimum, then throws everything left over at the top-priority debt. When that debt is gone, its payment rolls into attacking the next one — that’s how the snowball (or avalanche) accelerates. Avalanche orders by highest APR first, which costs the least in interest; snowball orders by smallest balance first, which clears debts fastest for quick wins. With mostly 0% medical debt, the two strategies often land within dollars of each other. This is a planning aid, not financial advice — confirm payment-plan terms with each provider before you commit.
