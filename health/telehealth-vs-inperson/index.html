Before you decide

  • Some plans charge the same copay for virtual and office visits — when they do, travel and time decide it.
  • The travel number is yours to set: gas, parking, tolls, or a rideshare fare.
  • Time has a real value: an hourly wage times hours missed is a fair estimate — including the waiting room.
  • Cost-sharing varies by plan. Confirm your telehealth and office-visit copays before you book.
