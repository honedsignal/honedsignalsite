This is an educational checklist, not legal or insurance advice. Work from your itemized bill and explanation of benefits (EOB) — summaries hide errors. If a charge looks wrong, don't pay it under pressure: ask the billing office to pause collection while it's reviewed, and get names, dates, and reference numbers for every call. Appeal deadlines vary by plan and state — check your plan documents and act quickly. Billing and coding rules differ for Medicare and Medicaid, so confirm which rules apply to your coverage.
