Before you decide

  • COBRA is time-limited — usually 18 months — and it's typically the most expensive option. It buys you continuity: same doctors, same network, no reset deductible mid-year.
  • Losing job coverage triggers a special enrollment period (usually 60 days) to join a marketplace plan outside open enrollment. Don't miss the window.
  • Check subsidies at healthcare.gov — many people qualify for premium tax credits that cut the marketplace price far below COBRA.
  • General guidance, not advice. This is a cost comparison, not a recommendation — confirm plan details with the marketplace or your benefits administrator.

Annual total = 12 months of premiums + estimated cost-sharing. Cost-sharing is simplified: you pay the year's medical spending up to the deductible in full, then 20% coinsurance on spending above it. Real plans vary — copays, separate drug deductibles, and out-of-pocket maximums all shift the numbers, and spending estimates are guesses, not predictions.
