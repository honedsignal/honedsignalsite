Five rules for every call

Call the number on the bill — not the hospital’s main line.The billing department is the team that can actually adjust your account. The front desk usually can’t.
Write down names, dates, and reference numbers.Every call: who you spoke with, the date and time, and any confirmation or reference number. This turns “they said” into a record.
Get it in writing before you pay.A discount or plan isn’t real until it’s documented — an email, a portal message, or a letter. Pay after you have it, not before.
Polite and persistent beats angry.Reps help calm callers more. And the first “no” often isn’t final — asking “is there anything else available?” or calling back another day works more often than you’d expect.
Never give payment details until there’s an agreement.Discuss the terms first, get them confirmed in writing, then pay. Card numbers are leverage — don’t hand them over while you’re still negotiating.
