Before you decide

  • Cash usually doesn't count toward your deductible. If you're close to meeting it, the insurance path may win even when it looks pricier today.
  • Get the cash price in writing before the appointment — quotes over the phone have a way of changing.
  • HSA or FSA funds can pay either way, so the tax advantage doesn't pick a side.
  • Near your out-of-pocket max? Run it through insurance — every covered dollar gets you closer to $0 cost-sharing.

This compares two estimates: the cash price you enter and your out-of-pocket cost through insurance (remaining deductible, then your coinsurance share of the allowed amount, plus any copay). Plans vary — some apply the copay instead of the deductible, some count cash payments toward the deductible, and the real allowed amount may differ from the estimate. Confirm the details with your insurer and the provider before you book. This is a planning aid, not insurance advice.
