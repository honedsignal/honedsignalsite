Before you book

  • Get the cash price in writing before the appointment — phone quotes have a way of changing.
  • Ask what's included. Imaging quotes sometimes exclude the radiologist's reading fee — ask, so there's no second bill.
  • Hospital outpatient departments are usually the priciest setting for the same test. Independent imaging centers tend to be cheapest.
  • Ask about pay-at-the-time-of-service discounts. Some facilities cut the cash price further if you pay up front.
