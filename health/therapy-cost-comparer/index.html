What each path usually looks like

  • In-network: you pay the copay per session; the therapist bills your insurer for the rest.
  • Out-of-network: you usually pay the full fee upfront, then file a claim and get reimbursed at your plan's percentage — reimbursement can take weeks.
  • Sliding scale: a reduced fee some therapists offer based on income; availability and eligibility vary by practice.
  • Therapy apps: a flat monthly subscription; many plans are message-based and may not include live sessions — check what the plan actually includes.

Monthly costs multiply your per-session price by the number of sessions you entered; the app price is a flat monthly fee that does not change with session count. The out-of-network number subtracts your plan's reimbursement percentage from the fee — confirm that percentage and any deductible requirements with your insurer before relying on it. This comparison covers cost only; it says nothing about quality, fit, or clinical approach, and is not medical advice.
