Good to know

  • Check your EOB. This tracker is only as accurate as what you enter — compare it against your plan's Explanation of Benefits after each claim.
  • Copays may not count. Some plans exclude copays from the deductible. Uncheck "counts toward deductible" for those.
  • HSA money works here. These are qualified medical expenses — you can pay them with HSA or FSA funds. Keep your receipts.
  • Not insurance advice. This is a tracking aid. Your plan documents and your insurer have the final word.

Enter your plan's deductible and out-of-pocket numbers from your summary of benefits, add each covered family member, and log every bill you pay. Per-member bars track spending that counts toward the deductible; family totals combine everyone. Out-of-pocket-max bars count everything you paid, including copays that don't count toward the deductible. Your data is stored only in this browser (localStorage) — nothing is sent anywhere, and exporting a CSV gives you a backup.
