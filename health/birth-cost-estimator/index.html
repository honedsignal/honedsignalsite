What drives the number

  • Facility fees — the room, staff, and equipment. Hospital charges dwarf birth-center charges.
  • Anesthesia / epidural — billed separately at many hospitals; can add hundreds to over a thousand dollars.
  • Length of stay — hospitals bill per night, and C-sections typically mean more nights.
  • NICU — if the baby needs neonatal intensive care, costs can rise far above these ranges.

This runs your plan's cost-sharing math twice: once on the low end and once on the high end of the typical facility-charge range for your scenario. You pay the deductible first, then your coinsurance percentage of the rest, with the total capped at your out-of-pocket max. These are estimates, not quotes — actual bills vary by provider, region, and how your insurer prices the claim, and separate professional bills (anesthesia, pediatrician) may arrive on their own.
