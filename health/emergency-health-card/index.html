        Review after every medication change.
        • Keep it current. Review the card after every medication change, new diagnosis, or insurance switch — an out-of-date card can mislead.
        • It supplements, never replaces, a medical ID bracelet or a formal advance directive. Wearables are seen first; paper is the backup.
        • Print at 100% scale (no "fit to page") so the card cuts out at true wallet size: 3.375 × 2.125 inches.
        • Privacy: your data never leaves this browser. But a printed card is physical — if your wallet is lost or stolen, whoever finds it sees your medical details. Carry it deliberately.
