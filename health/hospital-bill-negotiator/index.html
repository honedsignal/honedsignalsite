    Email letter

    Hospital bills are rarely take-it-or-leave-it: most hospitals offer self-pay discounts, financial-assistance ("charity care") programs, and interest-free payment plans. The letter asks for an itemized bill first (so you can check for errors), then a cash-pay discount, then financial assistance, with a payment plan as the fallback. Keep copies of everything, and get every agreement in writing. This is a planning aid, not legal advice.
