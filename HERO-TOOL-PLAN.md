# Honed Money — Hero Tool per Section (DRAFT)

**Concept (Freddie's, 2026-10-08):** Each section gets one hero calculator (the main
attraction, the front door) plus 2-3 supporting tools that point the user toward
their next decision. Reduces choice paralysis; creates a guided journey per section.

**Status:** Proposed, awaiting Freddie's review. Not yet built.

---

## Section hero map

### Auto (5 tools)
- **Hero:** Auto Loan Calculator — the core "what will this cost me" question
- **Support:** Lease vs Buy Car Calculator (buying direction), Auto Insurance Deductible Comparison (insurance direction)

### Budget (3 tools)
- **Hero:** 50/30/20 Budget Calculator — the simplest entry point
- **Support:** Monthly Budget Planner, Paycheck Budget Planner

### Business (8 tools)
- **Hero:** Break-Even Calculator — "will this business survive" is the #1 question
- **Support:** Business Loan Calculator, Profit Margin Calculator

### Career (4 tools)

### Credit (3 tools)
